## Race format (agreed direction)

### Enduro structure

- Use **existing labelled Glentress trails** — no custom trail building, no man-made features required
- **~6 timed segments** for the main enduro (exact trails TBC after scoping)
- **Friday + Saturday:** long open practice windows on all segments
- **Sunday:** **one timed attempt per segment** — "actual rally" feel
- Segment list revealed morning of (map / virtual map) — riders practice as many as they can before cutoff
- **Split start groups** — e.g. half to Left Valley first, half to Right Valley — reduces queues
- **No pro/amateur categories** — felt like it put people off at FTH
- **Per-segment prizes** as well as overall — people excelled on individual segments at FTH
- **Times hidden until prize giving** — builds suspense, reduces Adam stress, encourages mingling
  - Optional: times available via QR scan after prize giving, or printed receipts as memento
- **No in-person walkthroughs** — trails are clearly marked; optional virtual video per segment
- **No walking up mountains in full gear** for "walkthroughs"

### Timing systems discussed

1. **Adam's system** — limited gates at once; manual input; good for team endurance
2. **Hermes / phone GPS** — unreliable under trees; cheating concerns; no deletion
3. **SportIdent (SI)** — used by Glentress 7; beacons + cards; wrist not ankle (motor interference); marshal staggers starts; flexible multi-trail; **preferred direction** if cost works
4. **Glentress may already have SI infrastructure** on some trails

### Practice & schedule philosophy

- **10am start** on race days — not 8am after drinking
- Briefing on loop (recorded, plays every 30 min) OR website briefing — **not mandatory attendance**
- Trails open by 10:30 regardless
- Arrival day: register (online or in person), chill — no forced activities
- **No organised group rides** — provide suggested routes map only; James can lead optional rides
- Evening: Peebles pub ride along paths, fire pit, bingo, collaborative playlist

## Festival / social elements

### Hub & layout (Glentress)

- **Covered area near toilets** — main social hub (chairs! FTH had none)
- **Skills area flat** — brands, first aid, organisers, board demos
- **Hill after bike shop** — food vendors
- **Bottom field** — campers (500+ normal for Scottish Downhill / Glentress 7)
- **Campsite** — hookup, tents, pods; ~6 showers; toilet block showers during opening hours
- **Forest getaway cabins** — posh option at top of hill (far from social hub)

### Activities

- **Bingo** (not raffle) — 3-tier prizes (line 1/2/3), weird small prizes, mingling
- **Unicorn scavenger hunt** — hidden at suggested spots, local businesses (pubs, Go Ape), event wristband required
- **Board demos** — for passing MTB riders / spectators
- **Trail clinics** — liked at FLF; virtual video guides preferred over in-person
- **Charging** — plan ahead with Glentress; bike shop charging; partying vs venue relationship
- **Food trucks** — 2 food + 1 drink, or combined; local vendor profit share
- **Merch** — sell at Portugal trip and Glentress scoping weekends
- **Collaborative playlist** — each ticket buyer adds ~3 songs
- **No DJ** — too much money/fuss
- **No live stream / drone** — blurry, battery changes, rain; still photos better (Emilie)
- **Fire pit / BBQ** — campsite has communal fire pits
- **Karaoke** — discussed, noise limits TBC with venue

### Local integration

- Business cards for local pubs/cafes (Mollie design)
- Email pubs ahead — keep good relations
- Map with ride times between: hub, cafes, camping, bike shop, trails, Peebles
- "Other things to do" doc — like FTH's but published **weeks ahead**, not day before

## Website & digital (Dan's scope)

### Must-haves from chat

- **Public pages:** schedule, ticket info, venue info, maps, trail guides, "things to do", FAQ
- **Login required for:** digital waiver, digital briefing (+ short quiz), ticket-linked registration
- **Virtual trail walkthrough videos** — per segment; available before event (watch on drive up)
- **Prerecorded briefing** — plays on loop at hub AND on website
- **Online waiver** — tick-boxes per section (OneWheel app style) but on **website not app**
- **Briefing quiz** — ~5 questions (trail open time, race day, name a trail, etc.) — must pass to get QR code
- **QR code** — unique per person; scanned at registration to get timing chip/band
- **Admin mode** — different background colour; access to todos, locked content, ops info
- **Firebase auth** — Google + email
- **Deploy on Vercel** from repo
- **Placeholder images** OK to start
- **Content tone:** simple, informative, not AI-sounding

### Registration flow (from chat)

1. Buy ticket (online)
2. Create account / log in
3. Complete waiver
4. Watch briefing + pass quiz
5. Receive QR code (email + account)
6. Arrival: scan QR → get chip/band
7. Optional: register dependents (kids) under parent account
