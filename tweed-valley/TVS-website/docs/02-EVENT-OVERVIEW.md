# Event overview — Tweed Valley Senduro

A simple guide to what TVS is and how the weekend works.

---

## What is it?

Tweed Valley Senduro is a multi-day OneWheel enduro weekend at Glentress Mountain Bike Resort in the Scottish Borders. It uses the centre's existing trail network — no custom builds, no weeks of prep.

Think: practice all weekend, one timed run per trail on race day, then prizes, bingo, and a fire.

**Abbreviation:** TVS  
**First edition target:** 2027

---

## Who is it for?

Anyone who rides a OneWheel and wants a proper trail enduro without the faff of past UK events. No pro/amateur split. If you're fast on one segment, you can win that segment.

Under-18s welcome with a parent or guardian (details in waiver flow doc).

---

## Weekend structure (draft)

### Day 0 — Arrival (Thursday/Friday)

- Check in online or at the hub
- Collect wristband/chip if you completed waiver + briefing online
- Set up camp or head to your pod/cabin
- Suggested routes and Peebles pub ride — no organised group ride

### Day 1 — Practice (Friday)

| Time | What |
|------|------|
| 10:00 | Briefing recording plays at hub (optional — also on website) |
| 10:30 | All practice segments open |
| All day | Ride, charge, board demos, food trucks, merch |
| Evening | Bingo, fire pit, playlist, optional Peebles trip |

### Day 2 — Practice (Saturday)

Same as Friday. Segments stay open. This is your time to learn the trails.

Morning of: segment map published (website + hub). You get a map showing which trails count and suggested group splits (e.g. Left Valley first vs Right Valley first).

### Day 3 — Race (Sunday)

| Time | What |
|------|------|
| 10:00 | Briefing loop starts |
| 10:30 | **One timed run per segment** — times recorded, not published |
| Afternoon | Team race, endurance race |
| Evening | Prize giving (times revealed), fireside |

### Day 4 — Departure (Monday)

- Pack up, head home
- Times and photos on website after event

---

## Race format in plain terms

1. **~6 trails** are timed segments. They're normal Glentress trails — Spicy Tuna, Twitcher, Smells Like Tweed Spirit, etc. (final list after scoping).
2. **Practice** Fri–Sat as much as you want.
3. **Race day:** one shot per trail. Miss your window or crash? That's your run.
4. **Times hidden** until prize giving — you'll know if someone beat the leader on a segment, but not always who.
5. **Prizes:** overall winners + fastest per segment.
6. **Team race** and **endurance race** on Sunday — separate from the main enduro.
7. **Bonus:** complete 10 trails over the weekend (6 race + 4 bonus names drawn on final day) for an extra prize.

## Side activities

- Unicorn scavenger hunt (pubs, Go Ape, local businesses)
- Board demos for passing cyclists
- Trail guide videos on the website
- Bingo with small/weird prizes
- Suggested "other things to do" around the Tweed Valley

---

## What we're not doing

- Pro/amateur categories
- Head-to-head slalom (trails too tight)
- Trick competition
- Mandatory 8am briefing
- Mountain hike walkthroughs
- Live drone stream
- Separate event app to download
