# Organisation

Onewheel Scotland is a rider community with a public name and a shared WhatsApp community. Jess was going to look into a community interest company in October 2026. As of the last planning message on 18 October 2026, that paperwork is not described as filed. (EOL plan)

## How the community is arranged

- Onewheel Edinburgh is the older local chat. Jess created it on 31 December 2023. Dan was added on 11 August 2024 as an honorary member, to answer when Jess could not, including VESC and Portugal questions. (Edinburgh)
- On 28 December 2024, after a Dundee ride where two riders turned up from Facebook and nobody had their numbers, Nico said they needed an Onewheel Scotland group chat. The same day Jess added the Edinburgh chat to a WhatsApp community called Onewheel Scotland. The idea, already suggested to Andy from the way Shred the Shires and Float the Highlands are set up, was a Scotland-wide community with local chats under it. (Edinburgh)
- Andy Lakin created the Onewheel Scotland Group Rides chat on 28 December 2024. It is the Scotland-wide ride board: Edinburgh, Glasgow, Fife, Dundee, Aberdeen, the Highlands. People join from the community. (Group rides)
- Nico created the VESC and board-building chat the same day, inside the community, so technical talk would leave the main chat. (Board building; Edinburgh)
- A Facebook group called Onewheel Scotland is used for ride events. In August 2024 Jess made a new one, then found an older group and moved events there. She and Andy are admins of that older group. (Edinburgh, 20–21 Aug 2024)
- Ride news also goes out as WhatsApp events, because some people are not on Facebook. (Group rides, Mar 2025)

There is no constitution, membership list, or subscription in any of the chats.

Other people are named in the chats as possible help. None of those offers are confirmed: social media, website backup, photography, commentary support, campsite introductions, marshalling, audio, lighting. Leave them off a public team page until someone has actually taken the job.

## Companies that are not this one

Andy asked the group rides chat on 13 November 2025 whether an existing community interest company should spend its funds on individual racers, including a director. The replies, from Jess, Nico, Lee, James, and Katie, were that community money should be published against public aims, and that paying a director looks like a conflict. That company is the float-events body Andy already sits on. It is not Onewheel Scotland, and the site must not borrow its company number, its bank, or its aims.

Jess’s case for an Onewheel Scotland community interest company, in the planning chat, is practical: limited liability, a name an insurer or a landowner can contract with, a place for grants, and a body that continues if one organiser stops. She was clear that a company does not replace insurance. The paperwork is about funds not being spent for private gain. They would rather break even than post a profit. A test they used on spending ideas was whether the cost was real and whether leftover stock would be left behind. Merch, if it happens, should be ordered against money already taken.

Who would be directors, what the registered address would be, and whether a bank account would move from the organisers’ current arrangement are all undecided. The chats mention that Jess and Mollie can see the planning money. That is an internal fact. It does not belong on a public page.
