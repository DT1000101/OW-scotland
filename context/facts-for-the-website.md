## What the community does

- Riders across Scotland organise informal group rides: trail centres and scenic paths, with the pace set so new riders and smaller boards can finish the day. (Edinburgh; Group rides)
- The meeting habit is a named place and time, a session, food, a charge, and another session. (Group rides, Feb 2025 Glentress and Dundee plans)
- People who already ride help someone have a first go, on a beach, a cycle path, or an easy trail. (Edinburgh)
- The community includes people who build and repair boards, including VESC boards from kits such as Fungineers and Floatwheel. Experienced riders help with setup. The site does not publish build instructions. (Board building)
- On trails and at organised riding, the kit the group treats as normal is a helmet and wrist guards, with knee protection common among people who ride steep trails. League events check helmets. (Edinburgh; Group rides; EOL plan)
- Riders yield to walkers, cyclists, and horses, and they slow down around dogs. (Edinburgh)
- The club joins Forestry and Land Scotland visitor-service trail days in the Tweed Valley. The public booking page is https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/o/forestry-and-land-scotland-visitor-services-tweed-valley-34105789963. (EOL plan)

## The race, only at this strength

- The group is working toward a Scottish round of the European Onewheel League at Glentress, presented by Onewheel Scotland, under the event name Tweed Valley Senduro. (EOL plan)
- The aim is a first event in 2027, run as a small, marshalled race on existing mountain-bike trails, one rider at a time, in the built direction, with no new features. (EOL plan)
- They have opened a conversation with Forestry and Land Scotland. Onewheels are classed as motorised. A national way for members of a governing body to use Forestry land was discussed as future work. Permission for the event has not been granted in these chats. (EOL plan, 14 Oct 2026)
- A closed-trail event needs a Section 11 order from Scottish Borders Council, a risk assessment, and public liability insurance. (EOL plan)
- The event has its own site and its own Instagram, @tweedvalleysenduro. Details on that site were still temporary when the league was told about it. (EOL plan, 18 Oct 2026)
- Neighbouring UK events in the same sport include Float the Highlands at Glencoe and Shred the Shires. Onewheel Scotland is not those organisations. (EOL plan; Group rides)

## Sentences that match the risk assessment

Usable wording, taken from what they agreed to tell Forestry:

- Riders use single-wheeled self-balancing electric boards.
- The course is existing mountain-bike trail, ridden in the intended direction.
- One rider is on a race trail at a time.
- The board drives when the rider is on the footpads. Lifting the nose or coming off the board is how the motor is cut.
- Anyone on a board wears a helmet and wrist guards.
- Demonstrations are supervised.
- Riders give way to other people on the hill.

## Tone

Write like a host who has done the paperwork. Short sentences. No private jokes, no medical stories, no claim that a fee, a date, a sponsor, or a permit is agreed unless this file says it is.
