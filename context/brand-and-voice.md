# Brand and voice

- Enquiries: onewheelscotland@gmail.com
- Tagline: “No stress, just stoke”
- Line under that: “Fungineertech powered adventures through Scotland”

The word Onewheel is the brand riders use for the board. In formal sentences to a landowner they have also used “single-wheeled self-balancing electric boards”, because that is the description a risk assessment can carry. (EOL plan)

## How they want to sound

The public voice in the chats, once the jokes are taken out, is practical and friendly. New people are told to come, and that the ride will be set to the slowest boards and the newest riders. Jess’s line to a nervous new rider in Edinburgh was to use bike paths and quiet back roads, slow down around dogs, and wear a helmet. Nico’s Glentress invitation to a parent with children named the easy trails and the harder ones separately, so nobody would be surprised. (Edinburgh, Mar 2025 and Oct 2025)

They notice how they look to people who have never seen a board. Full-face helmets and covered faces read as intimidating on a city path. On a trail day the same helmet is the normal kit. The public site can say both things: protective kit on the trails, and a slow, visible, courteous style wherever other people are walking. (Edinburgh, Aug 2024 and Mar 2026)

The private chats are crude. That register stays in the chats. The line they actually enforce on each other in public is about not swarming a town, not leaving a board in the road, and yielding. A planning-chat shorthand for that was a private insult used as a house rule. The public version is the tagline Dan already chose.

Scottish detail they like, when it is about the event rather than the organisation: Irn-Bru, Tunnock’s or tablet, tweed colours, flat trophies, a map of Scotland. They argued against costume. Mollie distinguished “Onewheel Scotland” phrasing from Float the Highlands phrasing when someone borrowed a Highlands line. (Edinburgh, Oct 2025; EOL plan)

## Marks that are not the current logo

In August 2024 Jess made a Facebook-group image: a puffin in a helmet on a onewheel, assembled in PowerPoint because the image tool could not draw the board. Andy was going to make stickers. Dan exported a version with no background for people to put on posts. Nico’s joke at the time was that the Midlands mark was a pigeon and Scotland’s was a puffin. That was a Facebook-group graphic. It is not the logo file Dan has now supplied, and it should not be revived unless someone decides to. (Edinburgh, 25 Aug 2024)

For Tweed Valley Senduro, a separate mark was in progress in October 2026 and was not finished. The decisions that had settled:

- Green and orange as the event colours. Teal was discussed as a third colour. Mollie was wary of teal because she associates it with Float the Highlands.
- A map of Scotland, an orange pin at Glentress, large words. A onewheel rail through the O was debated and needs the rail-maker’s permission if their graphic is used. Fungineers were named. There is no free use of their rail in a logo.
- The puffin came off the event mark so the mark would stay simple.
- “Powered by Onewheel Scotland” as a secondary line, not the main event name.
- Hand-drawn rather than generated. An SVG with a transparent background, because WhatsApp turns files into JPEGs. The date sits at the bottom once the date is real.
- Mollie preferred one of two drawings. The export does not include the images, so the choice cannot be reconstructed from the text.

The event Instagram is @tweedvalleysenduro. The organisation Instagram is the one Dan confirmed.
